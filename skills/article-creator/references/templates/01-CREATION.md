# CREATION CHECKLIST

This checklist must be completed during the initialization of the article.

## Opening
- [ ] Verify that the article folder structure has been correctly initialized.
- [ ] Fill out the frontmatter in `ARTICLE.md` (Title, Bug, CTA, Intent, Platform, PublishDate).

## Closing
- [ ] Ensure the intent statement is detailed enough (at least 500 characters).
- [ ] Complete the list of initial sources in `SOURCES.md`.
